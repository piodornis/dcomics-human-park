Designstatus: EXPLORATION. Konkrete Anlage, Bauweise und Anordnung: PROPOSAL.
Werkzeug: eingebautes image_gen, Generierung mit zwei Stilreferenzen.
Referenzen: Earth B und Energieversorgung im Eis V4, im Gespräch für die weitere Earth-Entwicklung SELECTED. Kein projektweit freigegebener Style Pack.
Projekt-Repo dcomics-human-park unverändert; frühere Bilder bleiben erhalten.

## Fokus und Sichtprüfung

Nahansicht eines geschützten äußeren Wartungsgangs mit geöffnetem Serviceschrank, austauschbaren Modulen, sichtbaren Anschlussleisten, Rohrflanschen, Ventil und Anzeige. Kaltes Außenlicht und warme Betriebsbeleuchtung führen die Bildsprache von B/V4 fort. Technische Komponenten bleiben trocken, Schnee liegt am äußeren Gangrand.
Die klare Trennung von stark gealtertem Gehäuse und jüngeren Austauschbauteilen könnte in einer Folgefassung deutlicher werden: Die erste Fassung verteilt Gebrauchsspuren relativ breit. Die geöffnete Schranktür ragt in den Gang; freie Durchgangsbreite und Wartungsraum sind bei konkreter Ortsausarbeitung zu prüfen. Diese Ansicht ist ein Stiltest, keine technische Ausführungsplanung.

## Grenzen und Handoff

Upstream: project.md, design-project.md, canon/series-bible.md und issues/issue-001/script.md im Projekt-Repo. KANON ist die technisch weiter aktive, fortlaufend gewartete Erde. Dieses konkrete Design schafft keine neue bestätigte Weltregel und legt weder B-21s Arbeitsort noch die Kryo-Fundanlage fest.
Workflow: Comic Style Director aus comic-framework-design; narrative Zustände aus comic-framework-story; Modulgrenzen aus comic-framework-docs. Konkrete Ortsgeometrie und Folgeansichten gehen nach Auswahl an Comic Environment Designer.

## Exakter Prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: Human Park Earth maintenance-area close view, EXPLORATION style test, not a final location sheet.
Input Image 1: Earth data center B, SELECTED visual direction. Input Image 2: Earth energy landscape V4, SELECTED visual direction. Both are style/material/palette references ONLY; do not reproduce their exterior compositions, pylons, turbines or satellite sky.
Create a new single wide landscape comic illustration of a sheltered technical maintenance bay within Earth's still-operating ancient infrastructure. View from standing eye height, close enough to read practical assembly and repair history. No characters.
Narrative constraints: Earth is officially deserted but machinery and data infrastructure are continually maintained. Old parts are replaced and reconstructed. This particular bay is a visual PROPOSAL; it is NOT established as the cryogenic discovery site and must contain no cryopods or story clues.
Scene: a substantial wall-mounted service cabinet is the main subject, with one hinged access door open revealing a comprehensible arrangement of replaceable equipment drawers, a small terminal strip, neatly routed secured cables and accessible mechanical latches. Adjacent closed covers show three generations of maintenance: original rounded steel housing, bolted rectangular replacement cover in slightly different gray-blue metal, a smaller well-fitted repair patch with visible weld seam. A short pair of thick pipes connects through clearly drawn flanges to a compact pump/filter unit, with one accessible shutoff valve and a small analog dial. Pipes have purposeful continuous paths, not decorative spaghetti. Modular panels are sized to be removed through the access opening; door hinges and swing clearance make sense.
Spatial composition: foreground cabinet and service fittings dominate one side, a clear unobstructed grated maintenance walkway recedes past them into a shallow service passage. Plenty of working clearance in front of the open cabinet. A few overhead cable trays and support brackets frame depth without crowding. The main cabinet assembly fills enough of the frame for joints and material differences to be legible. No sprawling room layout or cutaway sheet.
Lighting and palette: mostly cold steel blue/cyan light entering from an off-frame external opening; violet-blue shadow recesses. One restrained warm amber maintenance luminaire and a few small amber operating indicators mark ongoing service. Ice/frost confined to the cold threshold and outer seams; working interior equipment is dry and maintained. No snow filling the open electronics.
Material storytelling: worn paint on edges and frequently handled latches, old screws with newer replacements, repaired seams, layered compatible components. Wear is selective, not all-over rust or catastrophic damage. Robust and serviceable after many maintenance cycles. Use small scuffs and careful patches to show care. No debris or abandoned tool piles.
Style: match references' detailed confident black comic ink; stronger outer contours, fine functional inner lines, hard-edged graphic shadows, separated saturated-but-controlled cold colors, crisp limited hatching. Detail strongest at repair interfaces, calmer broad cabinet planes and simplified rear passage. Hand-drawn stylized industrial comic, not photorealistic, not glossy 3D or painterly.
Avoid: robots, humans, animals, loose props, vacuum cleaners, weapons, chairs, cryopods, holograms, magical energy, glowing neon blue cables, pervasive hazard stripes, giant control screens, text, labels, letters, numbers, logos, panel borders, watermarks. No new narrative event. Single coherent close environment view.
