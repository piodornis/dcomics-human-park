## Prüfung durch Comic Style Director

Die kalte Blau/Cyan/Violett-Palette und sparsamen warmen Betriebslichter verbinden den Landschaftstest mit B. Wartungsspuren sind vor allem an Vordergrundgehäusen und Geländern sichtbar. Kraftwerk, Windanlagen und Stromtrasse vermitteln fortgesetzten technischen Betrieb. Die Stromtrasse führt zu fernen Rechenzentren und macht den funktionalen Zusammenhang lesbar.

Weiter zu prüfen: Der Entwurf verwendet deutlich heutige Kraftwerks-, Mast- und Windturbinenformen. Ob diese historische Wiedererkennbarkeit oder eine stärkere Weiterentwicklung über viele Reparaturgenerationen gewünscht ist, bleibt eine Designentscheidung. Satellitenbänder sind stark grafisch stilisiert; ihre räumliche Darstellung ist nicht festgelegt. Keine technische Ausführungsplanung, keine endgültige Ortsgeometrie.

## Exakter Prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: Human Park — Earth energy landscape, a bounded comic style test, EXPLORATION.
Input Image 1: selected style reference B. Use ONLY its confident black ink, blue/cyan/violet palette, sparse amber operational lights, weathered repaired materials and graphic shading. Create a NEW landscape composition, do not reproduce its dominant data-center facade.
Narrative constraints: Earth is officially deserted yet enormous data centers, nuclear power plants, wind installations and old maintenance infrastructure continue working. Dense orbital satellites, relays and archive modules partly obscure the sky. No humans, no robots required. This concrete location is a visual proposal, not an established place.
Scene: wide frozen plain seen from a modest elevated maintenance embankment, an ancient but still operating power district spanning a vast icy valley. Recognizable low nuclear containment buildings and turbine halls in the middle distance, with several tall old wind turbines farther across the plain. A legible high-voltage transmission route links the power station to distant vertical data-center blocks at the horizon. Electrical pylons have believable hanging power cables and insulators, not glowing fantasy energy beams. Functional installations have been maintained through generations: patch panels, replacement braces and mismatched service covers, snow-capped but standing securely. Minimal narrow pale vapor at operational exhausts, no pollution apocalypse.
Composition: panoramic landscape, open space and long perspective make distance palpable. A close foreground utility housing and weathered steel maintenance railing supply tactile repair detail and human-readable scale without characters; a restrained amber lamp marks the access route. The route leads toward the middle-distance generating station; pylons draw the eye onward toward the small distant data-center silhouettes. Turbines are spaced plausibly, not a forest of identical poles. Plenty of breathing room across the blue ice; keep foreground, station, distant industry distinctly separated.
Sky: indigo and violet twilight, a dense orbital archive shell rendered as many tiny distant technical silhouettes and dots in layered curved belts with only a few individually readable satellites. Avoid physically drawn connecting orbit lines, giant low-flying satellites or toy drones.
Rendering: detailed hand-inked comic illustration consistent with reference B, heavier outer contours and fine interior linework, hard-edged graphic shadows, restrained hatching and crisp wear marks. High detail on near repair seams and hardware, medium detail at the plant, simplified far silhouettes; never equal microdetail across every depth. Cold cyan ice, cobalt and violet shadow, sparse warm amber windows and maintenance beacons. Quiet, immense, diligently maintained despite age; no generic rubble wasteland.
Avoid: characters, robots, cleaning tools, vehicles, vegetation, text, labels, branding, panel borders, watermarks, explosions, neon beams, holograms, photorealism, painterly rendering. Single coherent wide image, no collage.
